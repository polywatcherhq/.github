# Polywatcher

**Real-time whale positioning on Polymarket and Hyperliquid perpetual futures, all in one chart. Trading integration within the same chart coming soon.**

Polywatcher is for traders on Polymarket Perps and Hyperliquid who intend to follow the money (confluences) using our proprietary indicators whenever entering/closing positions. It shows every whale order of $50K or more on the chart, with liquidations, walls and positioning, all in one chart.

## On the chart

- **Whale orders:** every order of $50K or more on Polymarket Perps and Hyperliquid, marked at its time and price.
- **Liquidations:** every Polymarket Perps liquidation, marked at the price where it hit.
- **Long and short walls:** how much money the whales hold long and short in each price range. A long wall is where the most money went long and is still in. A short wall is where the most money went short and is still in.
- **Net positioning:** whether the whales on an asset hold more long positions or more short positions, and how that changes over time.
- **The Spread:** the Polymarket Perps price against the Hyperliquid price for the same asset.
- **Funding, open interest and whale leverage,** next to the price.

## Lists

- **Live Trades:** every Polymarket Perps order of $1K or more, and every Hyperliquid order of $50K or more.
- **Whales:** every whale order of $50K or more on both platforms.
- **Liquidations:** Polymarket Perps liquidations with their realized P&L.
- **Leaderboard:** the top Polymarket Perps traders for Today, Week, Month and All time.
